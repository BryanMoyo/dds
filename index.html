Nouveau Royale

"Dance to Express, Not to Impress"

Founded in 2015 by twin sisters Lakiesha and Natisha Claxton, Nouveau Royale began as a passionate dance group of nine members and has blossomed into a thriving dance school built on artistry, discipline, and love for the craft.

Our Story

A legacy of movement, passion, and community

More Than Movement

At Nouveau Royale, dance is more than movement — it's expression, confidence, and community. Our mission is to inspire dancers of all ages and levels to discover the joy of dance while building lifelong skills.

What started as a small team with big dreams soon grew into a family of dancers, bonded by artistry, discipline, and love for the craft. Over the years, that passion has blossomed into the thriving dance school we are today, carrying forward the same energy and vision that sparked our journey.

Our Classes

Professional training for every age and skill level

Performance Ensemble

Flagship Program

The heart of our school. Students come together to build skills in teamwork, stage presence, and artistry while preparing for concerts and showcases. In addition to main studio performances, members volunteer for extra opportunities throughout the year.

Ballet

$75/month

The foundation of all dance. Students will develop grace, posture, strength, and discipline while learning classical ballet technique. This class supports performance quality across every style.

Acrobatics / Tumbling

$75/month

A structured progressive training pathway designed to develop strength, flexibility, agility, and performance artistry. Students begin with foundational skills such as rolls, handstands, and cartwheels, then progress safely into walkovers, aerials, and advanced tricks — all blended seamlessly with dance choreography.

Aerial Hoop

$80/month

Students train on a suspended hoop, developing strength, balance, and coordination. They will explore aerial techniques and creative sequences while building confidence and artistry in the air.

Aerial Silks

$80/month

Using fabric suspended from the ceiling, students learn climbs, wraps, drops, and poses. Aerial Silks helps students build strength, flexibility, and expressive artistry while exploring this exciting circus-inspired discipline.

Flexibility & Contortion

$55/month

This class focuses on safely increasing flexibility, balance, and body control. Students will work on deep stretching techniques, controlled movements, and contortion skills in a structured environment.

Our Founders

Visionaries behind the movement

LC

Lakiesha Claxton

Co-Founder, Creative & Artistic Director

Since 2015, Lakiesha has been instrumental in guiding the growth of Nouveau Royale, overseeing artistry and operations. She is certified in Ballet and Dance, and holds a certification in Business Management — a combination that allows her to merge creativity with organizational leadership.

As an active choreographer, she contributes original routines and works closely with dancers to bring performances to life. She specializes in choreography, performance planning, and artistic direction.

Certifications: Ballet, Dance, Business Management
NC

Natisha Claxton

Co-Founder, Creative & Artistic Director

Natisha leads the studio with passion, discipline, and a hands-on teaching approach. Since 2015, she has taken on multiple roles — owner, director, choreographer, and instructor — making her a cornerstone of the school's success.

She is celebrated for her innovative choreography and dynamic teaching style, which blend technical precision with creativity and expression. With her direct leadership in the studio, she embodies the school's motto, inspiring dancers to grow as both artists and individuals.

Certifications: Acrobatics, Aerial Hoop, Aerial Silks, Ballet, Tap
